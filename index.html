We design houses, halls and workplaces that get better with age.

Selected work

Our studio is fourteen people in a former printing works. We draw by hand first, build models for every project, and visit each site more often than the schedule asks us to.

Architecture

New buildings and extensions, from a single family house to a thirty-unit residential block. We lead the project from first sketch to the last snag list.

Interiors

Spaces fitted around the way people really work and live, with custom joinery, honest materials and light that changes through the day.

Reuse

Old factories, barns and offices given a second life. We keep what still works and add only what the building needs.
